# Judgment memory

Rules the agent has learned about how this user handles items. One rule per line, prefixed with `- `.

- Bulk promotions, coupons, re-engagement campaigns, product surveys, newsletters, job digests, and social-profile statistics or recommendations without a direct personal request — ignore
- Automated job-application acknowledgments or 'under review' updates that provide no decision or requested next step — ignore
- One-time login codes, routine invoice-availability notices without a payment request or billing problem, and repository release announcements without a requested change — archive
- Interview invitations requesting availability, confirmation, or selection of a time slot, including invitations delivered through automated recruiting systems — reply
- Email-confirmation or activation requests needed to join a workspace or enable an account, rather than messages merely supplying a login code — act
- Event or program organizer messages providing track details or participation expectations, rather than generic event promotion — act
- Informational organizer updates about an ongoing event or program—such as weekly recaps, countdowns, or gallery availability—with no concrete participant task requested — archive
- Organizer messages giving actionable track instructions or participation requirements that call for preparation, submission, or another concrete participant step — act
- Bulk event or program outreach aimed at recruitment, sales, or re-engagement, rather than reporting ongoing-program progress or providing participation instructions — ignore
- In an ongoing collaborator or client thread, a direct question or request to check, review, or clarify shared work, even if investigation is needed before responding — reply
- Updates changing a deadline or submission requirement for the user's assignment or pending deliverable, even without an explicit request to take action — act
- Informational event, program, or course updates limited to recaps, countdowns, or gallery availability, with no concrete participant task or change to the user's obligations — archive
